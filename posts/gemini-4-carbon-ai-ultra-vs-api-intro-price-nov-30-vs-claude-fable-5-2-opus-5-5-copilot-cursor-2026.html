Gemini 4 Carbon is out: Google AI Ultra or the $2/$10 API (until Nov 30), and is it a better buy than Claude Fable 5.2 or Opus 5.5? (Nov 2026)

On Nov 10, 2026, Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to everyone who pays, two weeks after giving it to a small group of cyber defenders. Google calls it its strongest model, for long, multi-step coding, research and agent work. Argon stays as the everyday model. It landed one day after Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2, so the question on X is the same one we answered for Fable: which plan gets you Carbon, what it costs on the API before the price doubles on Dec 1, and whether it beats Claude for the money. We read Google's launch blog, the Gemini API pricing and "what's new" docs, the Antigravity blog, 9to5Google, GitHub's changelog, Cursor's model docs and Artificial Analysis on the morning of Nov 11.

Quick verdict

You're on Google AI Pro ($19.99): you don't get Carbon. You keep Gemini 4 Argon, which Google says is still "the ideal choice for the majority of software engineering and knowledge work." Don't upgrade just for Carbon until you've hit Argon's ceiling. You're on Google AI Ultra ($99.99 5x or $199.99 20x): Carbon is rolling out to you in the Gemini app ("Most complex work" in the picker), AI Mode in Search, Antigravity and AI Studio. Google hasn't published Carbon message limits, and some Ultra 5x users on X say the app limits run out fast. You build on the API: gemini-4-carbon costs $2 in / $10 out per million tokens through Nov 30, then $4 / $20. Paid tier only, no free tier. Thinking tokens bill as output. You're choosing between Carbon and Claude: at list price, Carbon is a quarter of Fable 5.2's price ($8/$40) until Nov 30 and half of it after. On Artificial Analysis's index it costs about $7.76 per task against $26.54 for Fable 5.2, but Fable still scores higher (65 vs 60). After Dec 1, Carbon costs the same per token as Opus 5.5. You live in GitHub Copilot or Cursor: Carbon is in Copilot Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise (not Free or Pro) and in Cursor as a third-party model. Both bill it at Google's per-token rates, not from included usage.

What Google shipped on Nov 10

  • Who it's for: Google says Carbon is for "tasks that test the limits of a model's intelligence," like complex multi-step coding and debugging and open-ended research. It uses more tokens than Argon to explore, reason and verify. Argon stays the default pick for most work
  • Specs: 1M-token context, long output up to 1M tokens, thinking levels low, medium (default) and high at the same per-token price. Google recommends max_output_tokens of at least 32,768 and client timeouts of at least 15 minutes
  • API gotchas: explicit context caching isn't supported (implicit caching only, 95% off cached input on prompts of 4,096+ tokens). temperature, top_p and top_k are ignored. Every token is billed, including thinking tokens and grounding tool instructions
  • /swarm in Antigravity (preview): one prompt becomes a team of agents: a coordinator, parallel workers in separate git worktrees, and verifiers that run tests and challenge diffs. Rolling out in the Antigravity app and CLI for Ultra subscribers
  • Gemini app: 9to5Google reports the picker now shows 4 Argon ("Code & analysis") and 4 Carbon ("Most complex work"), and Gemini 3.1 Pro has been removed
  • Free for some scientists: researchers can apply for six months of free access to Argon and Carbon on Antigravity's highest usage tier, subject to eligibility and capacity
  • Read the cyber numbers carefully: Google's top security benchmark results are for Carbon for cyber through its Fairwind Program, not the public model

Which to pick

  • Solo developer on the API: try Carbon on your hardest jobs before Nov 30, while it's $2/$10. Cost out real tasks, then re-run the numbers at $4/$20 before you switch your default
  • Google AI Pro user who's happy with Argon: stay put. Upgrade to Ultra only if you need Carbon or /swarm every week
  • Claude Max/Ultra user: no reason to cancel. Fable 5.2 still leads Artificial Analysis's index, and it's included up to 50% of your weekly limit. Carbon via the API is a cheap second opinion for long agent runs
  • Copilot Pro user: Carbon and Fable 5.2 both need Pro+ or higher. Pro+ is the minimum tier for either
  • Security teams: the headline cyber scores come from Fairwind's Carbon for cyber. Apply there rather than judging the public model on those numbers
