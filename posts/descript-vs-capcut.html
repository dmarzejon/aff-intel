Descript vs CapCut

Two very different editors that both claim “fast content.” Descript is transcript-first; CapCut is timeline-first for social.

Quick verdict

Descript for podcasts, talking-head YouTube, and overdub/filler-word workflows. CapCut for Reels/TikTok/Shorts, templates, captions, and mobile-friendly speed.
