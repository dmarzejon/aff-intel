Claude Meetings (beta) vs ChatGPT Meetings and Record, Gemini in Meet, Granola, Otter, Fireflies and Fathom: who gets it, what it costs, and when a separate note-taker still makes sense (Nov 2026)

Anthropic started rolling out Claude Meetings in beta on Nov 12, 2026. Two weeks later it's one of the most shared AI clips on X: a man on a video call with four Claude tiles splitting up the work, and a run of "I cancelled Granola the day I got Claude Meetings" posts. Most of the replies ask the same thing: can I get it on Pro, and does it work in Zoom? The answer to both, for now, is no. Quick verdict

Your team is on Claude Team and meets in Google Meet: try it now. It's on by default for Team, and notes, transcripts and in-call answers cost nothing extra during the beta. Before the next client call, check one setting: on Team, Claude can join calls with outside guests by default. You're on Claude Pro, Max or Ultra: you can't use it. Claude Meetings is Team and Enterprise only. If you meet alone or with clients, a free tier from Granola or Fathom, or ChatGPT Plus's Record on a Mac, is the cheaper route. You meet in Zoom or Microsoft Teams: Claude Meetings supports Google Meet only ("other platforms coming soon"). Otter, Fireflies and Fathom all work in Zoom, Teams and Meet, and Otter is 50% off annual plans for Black Friday. You already pay for Google Workspace Business Standard or higher: Gemini in Meet (notes, summaries, translation) is already in your plan. Claude Meetings is for teams that want the doc, whiteboard and action items built during the call.

What Claude Meetings does

  • Joins as a visible participant in Google Meet, posts a notice in the call's chat, and shows a live tile with its current state
  • Answers questions when you say "Claude" and ask, or type in the Meet chat. Answers come back in writing in the Meet chat, which everyone on the call can see
  • Builds while you talk: "Claude, make a doc" or "make a whiteboard"; slides and other artifacts work too, and teammates on the invite can edit them live
  • Uses your connected tools (say, sending a Slack message) with each person's permission. It asks the first time, and a yes applies from then on. Spoken requests get a "who's speaking?" check each time for now
  • After the call: a meeting page in claude.ai with summary, action items, transcript and everything Claude made. Invitees from your organization get a recap email. You can ask Claude about past meetings later
  • Keeps text, not audio: it saves a transcript, a summary and screen descriptions. It can stay up to 8 hours and warns before it leaves
  • Joining: add it from Meetings in Claude after connecting Google Calendar (an owner has to enable Google Calendar first), or turn on auto-join for all meetings or all except one-on-ones. Owners can't switch on auto-join for you

Before you switch it on

  • Consent: Anthropic tells you to make sure everyone on the call is OK with being transcribed. Guests' words go into the notes and transcript even when Claude doesn't reply to them
  • Outside guests on Team: allowed by default. An owner can turn it off in your organization's settings
  • Connected tools: one "yes" lasts. If your workspace auto-connects people's apps, Claude can reach them from a call
  • The free period has an end date for artifacts (Feb 10, 2027), and Anthropic hasn't published post-beta pricing for notes. Don't cancel a paid note-taker on a multi-year contract yet
