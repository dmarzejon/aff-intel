Notion AI's new credit system: what 1,500 Standard + 500 Select credits buy on Business, what changes on Nov 2, and when ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro is the better buy (2026)

On Monday, Oct 19, Notion shipped its biggest Notion AI update in a while. Agents now get their own computer and browser and can run tasks for up to 24 hours. There's a new Auto model router, and you can connect a ChatGPT plan. It also put all Notion AI usage on a new credit system. Notion's announcement on X passed 83,000 views, and co-founder Akshay Kothari's post ("We've also overhauled our pricing…") passed 68,000. The credit change is the part that hits your bill. On Business and Enterprise, each member now gets 1,500 Standard credits and 500 Select credits a month. New workspaces are on the system from Oct 19. Existing workspaces switch on Nov 2, 2026. Below is what that allowance buys, how to stretch it, and when a $20 chat plan is the better spend. We read Notion's Oct 19 release notes, its pricing page, and its help articles on AI credits, Auto, Notion credits, Custom Agents, and the ChatGPT plan connection on Oct 20.

Quick verdict

Your team runs on Notion Business: leave Notion Agent on Auto. Auto spends Standard credits (1,500 a month). Notion estimates 5–25 credits per task on Auto vs 50–150 when you pick Opus 5.5 yourself. You like picking a frontier model in Notion: 500 Select credits is about 3 complex tasks or 10 simple ones on Opus 5.5 a month (our math from Notion's estimates). Beyond that, your admin buys Notion credits at $10 per 1,000. You already pay for ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Go: connect it. When you pick a GPT model in Notion Agent, the work draws on your ChatGPT plan instead of your Notion credits. This needs a Business or Enterprise workspace. You mainly want a strong model for long work: Claude Pro ($20, or $17/month annually) or ChatGPT Plus ($20) gives you rolling limits, not a fixed monthly credit count. Use Notion's Auto for in-workspace edits and summaries. You're on Notion Free or Plus: you only get a trial of Notion AI. What changed on Oct 19

  • One credit system for all AI usage: "we're bringing together all AI usage under a new credit system" (Notion release notes). Notion Agent, AI editing and blocks, Custom Agents, and (from Dec 1) AI Meeting Notes all draw from credits
  • Two per-member allowances on Business and Enterprise, both reset monthly with no rollover. They're separate: you can't combine them, swap one kind for the other, or move them between members
    • Standard: 1,500 a month. For work where Notion picks the model: Notion Agent on Auto (including chat), writing and translation, AI blocks, formulas, and inline editing. AI Meeting Notes join this bucket on Dec 1
    • Select: 500 a month. For work where you pick the model yourself, and for Custom Agents you set up for your own use
  • Notion credits (the shared workspace balance an admin buys) cover everything past those allowances, plus "high-credit-consumption models not included in your plan, such as Fable and Astra," workspace-funded Custom Agents, and agents run through the API or a connected app
  • When you run out, work pauses until the monthly reset, unless an admin turns on "Allow workspace to use Notion credits after AI limit is reached." That setting is off by default
  • Timing: new Business and Enterprise workspaces are on credits from Oct 19. Existing workspaces switch on Nov 2, 2026. Notion adds that "your included amount may vary depending on your plan and purchase arrangement," so check Settings → Notion AI → Usage
  • Also new: agents with their own computer and browser that can run up to 24 hours, a "Review for me" mode, a ChatGPT plan connection, BYOK (private alpha, Enterprise only), memory sync in Notion MCP, and an "AI Command Center" for usage and spend limits (coming soon)

How to make the credits last

  1. Leave Notion Agent on Auto for edits, summaries, translations, and database chores. Auto uses Standard credits, so your 500 Select credits stay free for the times you really want a specific model.
  2. Connect your ChatGPT plan (Settings → Notion AI → Usage → Connected subscriptions → Connect). Per Notion's docs, picking a GPT model then uses your ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Go allowance instead of Notion credits. Auto and non-GPT models still use Notion credits. It works in Notion Agent only, not Custom Agents. If your ChatGPT limit runs out, Notion asks before switching you to workspace credits.
  3. Admins: decide on overage before Nov 2. With the overage setting off (the default), people simply pause when they run out. With it on, plan a Notion credit budget and watch the dashboard (Settings → Access & billing → Notion credits).
  4. Fund shared Custom Agents from the workspace so one teammate's Select credits don't pay for everyone's agent runs.
  5. Do the long, heavy work in a chat plan. If you regularly want Opus-class output for long documents or research, a $20 Claude or ChatGPT plan with rolling limits is likely cheaper than buying Notion credits at $1.50 a task. Then paste the result into Notion and let Auto handle the formatting.
