ChatGPT Pro 200 usage halves on Oct 30: stay, drop to Pro 100, move to Pro 500, or switch to Claude Max or Kimi? (2026)

OpenAI announced it at DevDay on Sep 29, and it takes effect this Friday, Oct 30. If you had ChatGPT Pro 200 before the announcement, your included usage in ChatGPT Work and Codex drops from 20x to 10x the Plus allowance, and your GPT-6 Pro limit in Chat drops from 200 to 100 messages a week. The price stays $200 a month. You keep the old limits through Thursday, Oct 29. To soften it, OpenAI is giving existing subscribers a one-time 62,500 usage credits ("a $2,500 USD value") that expire on Dec 31, 2026. It's trending on X again this week, with Pro users posting low usage meters and saying they'll move to Claude Max. Below: what changes, the per-dollar math, a quick way to check whether 10x fits your week, and how Pro 100, Pro 500, Claude Max, and Kimi compare. We read OpenAI's, Anthropic's, and Moonshot's pages on Oct 28.

Quick verdict

You use less than half of your weekly Pro 200 meter today: stay put. On 10x, the same work should use about twice the share of the meter, so you still fit. Spend the $2,500 grant before Dec 31. You use less than a quarter of it: Pro 100 ($100, 5x Plus) would probably cover you. The rate per Plus allowance is now the same on every tier, so you lose nothing by going smaller. Wait until you've seen how the grant works on your account before you downgrade. You regularly use most of your 20x: 10x won't cover you. Your options are Pro 500 ($500, 25x Plus, plus Ultrafast), spending credits, moving routine work to GPT-6.1 Terra, or adding a second vendor such as Claude Max or Kimi. You're new to Pro: nothing changes for you. New Pro 200 subscriptions have used the lower allowance since DevDay.

What changes on Oct 30

  • Who's affected: people who had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from Sep 22, 2026 through 10 a.m. PT on Sep 29, 2026. OpenAI's Help Center says they "can use the previous included allowance through October 29, 2026," and then "your subscription will move to the lower included usage allowance. Your subscription price stays at $200/month"
  • Work and Codex: from 20x to 10x the ChatGPT Plus allowance. Work, Codex, and ChatGPT for Excel, Word, and PowerPoint all draw on that one allowance, so heavy Codex users feel it most
  • GPT-6 Pro in Chat: from 200 to 100 messages a week. On Pro 200, when you hit it, ChatGPT switches you to GPT-5.6 Thinking at Medium
  • No 5-hour limit: OpenAI's email says it is "committing to not reintroducing the 5hr limit," and its docs say all three Pro tiers "currently have no five-hour usage limit." You still have a weekly allowance
  • The grant: 62,500 usage credits ($2,500 value), shown in Settings > Usage, expiring Dec 31, 2026
  • Where the numbers come from: the dates, prices, and grandfathering rules are on OpenAI's Help Center. The 20x, 10x, and 25x multiples, the 200-to-100 cut, and the grant are from OpenAI's email to affected subscribers, as posted by recipients on Reddit, Hacker News, and paddo.dev. The current Help Center page only says Pro 200 "includes more usage than Pro 100." Pro 100's 5x is from OpenAI's earlier Pro tiers page ("5x higher limits than Plus"), and no newer OpenAI page restates it

Three ways to stretch 10x

  • Make GPT-6.1 Terra your default for routine work. It launched Oct 26. OpenAI's Plus estimates are 35–285 local messages per 5 hours on Terra vs 15–160 on GPT-6.1 Sol, and its credit rates are half of Sol's (25 / 1.25 / 125 credits per 1M input / cached / output tokens vs 50 / 2.5 / 250). For simple edits, browser tasks, and writing, that roughly doubles how far 10x goes. Keep Sol or Astra for hard coding. See GPT-6.1 Terra vs Sol, Claude, and Kimi
  • Spend the grant on overflow. Included usage goes first, then credits. OpenAI says "a typical GPT-5.6 Sol task may use 2-15 credits," and GPT-6.1 Sol's credit rates are half of GPT-5.6 Sol's. At that range, 62,500 credits is roughly 4,000 to 31,000 typical GPT-5.6 Sol tasks (our arithmetic). It's gone on Dec 31, so for November and December the cut may not bite much. January is when you'll feel it
  • Cut the context you send. OpenAI's own tips: trim prompts, keep AGENTS.md short (nest it per folder), and turn off MCP servers you aren't using. Each one adds context to every message

Which to pick

  • Grandfathered Pro 200, light-to-medium user: stay. Use the grant, and make Terra the default for routine tasks
  • Grandfathered Pro 200, under a quarter of the meter: Pro 100 is the same rate per allowance for half the price. Check in Settings > Usage that the grant is still there before you switch, because OpenAI hasn't said what happens to it if you change tiers
  • Heavy Codex user who lives at 80%+ of 20x: Pro 500 if you also want Ultrafast and don't want a second tool. Otherwise Pro 200 plus a second $100–$200 plan from another vendor gives you more total room and a backup model
  • Thinking about Claude Max 20x instead: it's the same $200 and adds $200 a month in API credits, but it has a 5-hour window and the open questions about weekly limits. Try Claude Pro ($20) on your real tasks for a week before you cancel anything. See Is Anthropic A/B testing Claude Max limits?
  • Want overflow without a weekly cap: Kimi Max ($99) or Ultra ($199). See Kimi K3.1 Pro plans
  • GPT-6 Pro chat power user (not Codex): 100 messages a week is the real cut for you, and credits don't add GPT-6 Pro messages. If 100 isn't enough, Pro 500 is OpenAI's only bigger option
