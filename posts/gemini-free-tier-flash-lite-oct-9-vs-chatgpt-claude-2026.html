Gemini’s free tier goes Flash-Lite only on Oct 9: pay for AI Plus or AI Pro, or switch to ChatGPT or Claude? (2026)

Google has published a Gemini Apps Help page called “Changes to Gemini model access and limits.” It says model availability for personal accounts changes in October 2026, and that the changes “will start to take effect for users without an AI subscription on October 9th.” After that, the free tier keeps Flash-Lite only. AI Plus keeps Flash-Lite and Flash but loses Pro, and Google says Plus subscribers “should receive an email that explains when these changes will take effect.” AI Pro and AI Ultra keep all three models. We read Google’s page on the morning of Oct 7. The buyer question is simple: if Gemini Pro is the model you rely on, which plan do you actually need on Friday, and is a different assistant a better use of the same $20?

Quick verdict

You use free Gemini for quick summaries, drafts, and brainstorming: stay free and try Flash-Lite for a week. Google pitches it for exactly those everyday tasks. You use free Gemini for coding, math, long PDFs, or study notes: you lose Pro and Flash on Oct 9. The cheapest Google plan that keeps Pro is Google AI Pro at $19.99/month ($199.99/year). AI Plus won’t do it, because Plus loses Pro too. You’re on AI Plus because it was the cheap way to get Pro: watch for Google’s email with your date. After it, Plus is a Flash plan. Either move up to AI Pro or compare it against ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro at the same price. You only need “more than free” for everyday chat: ChatGPT Go ($8/month) or AI Plus (reported at $4.99/month in the US) are the low-cost options. Neither one gets you Gemini Pro. Do not: upgrade because of the date alone. Test your real tasks on Flash-Lite first, and remember that the new “high” effort setting uses more of your limit on every plan.

What Google actually changed

  • Free (no plan): Flash-Lite only once the change reaches you. Google says it starts taking effect Oct 9. Flash and Pro leave the free model picker
  • AI Plus: Flash-Lite and Flash. Pro goes away on a per-account date Google sends by email, so not every Plus user switches on Oct 9
  • AI Pro and AI Ultra: Flash-Lite, Flash, and Pro, with no model removed
  • Effort levels: for each model you can pick low, medium, or high effort. Google says higher effort gives more thorough answers “but will also use more of your limit”
  • Deep Think: on Oct 7, Google’s change page and its limits page both list Deep Think for AI Ultra. Some early coverage (9to5Google, Oct 3) reported that AI Pro would also get Deep Think. We couldn’t confirm that on Google’s current pages, so don’t buy AI Pro for Deep Think yet
  • Unchanged: the compute-based limits from May still apply. Limits refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap: standard on free, 2x on Plus, 4x on Pro, and 5x or 20x Pro on Ultra. Context windows stay at 32k tokens free, 128k on Plus, and 1M on Pro and Ultra
  • Scope: this applies to Gemini Apps on personal Google accounts. Google’s notice doesn’t announce the same change for work or school accounts

Checklist before Friday

  1. Check which model you actually use. In Gemini, go to Settings and then Usage Limits to see your plan and usage. If you rarely leave Flash, the free change costs you less than it sounds.
  2. Re-run your three most important prompts on Flash-Lite now while you can still compare against Pro. Note the accuracy and how much you have to fix by hand.
  3. AI Plus subscribers: look for Google’s email with your effective date. Until it arrives you still have Pro, so this is the moment to test whether Flash is enough.
  4. Price the bundle, not just the chatbot. AI Pro includes 5 TB of storage and other Google One perks. If you already pay for Google storage, the real upgrade cost is lower. If you don’t care about storage, compare it straight against ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro.
  5. Coding users: if Gemini Pro was your free coding model, check whether you already have a coding agent seat (Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex) before paying for another subscription.
  6. Students and long-PDF users: free stays at a 32k context window. If you upload big files, the 1M context on AI Pro, or Claude Pro, matters more than which model name you get.
