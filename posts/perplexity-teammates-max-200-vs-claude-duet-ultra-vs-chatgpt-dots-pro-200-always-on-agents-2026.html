Perplexity Teammates ($200 Max) vs Claude Duet vs ChatGPT dots: which always-on AI agent is worth paying for (Nov 2026)

On Nov 4, 2026, Perplexity launched Teammates: named agents that own ongoing work, such as watching a Slack channel, triaging GitHub or Linear issues, or tracking a market, and that keep their role and memory between runs. The catch for buyers: the research preview is for individual Perplexity Max subscribers only, which is $200 a month or $2,000 a year, and the work is metered by Max's monthly Computer credits. That puts it head to head with the two agents the big labs already bundle: Claude Duet (included on US Pro, Max and Ultra) and ChatGPT dots (included on ChatGPT Pro from $100). We read Perplexity's launch post and Help Center, Anthropic's Duet help article, and our own earlier reads of OpenAI's dots pages on the morning of Nov 9.

Update, Nov 12: ChatGPT dots can now run on your own computer

Update, Nov 12: OpenAI added a ChatGPT desktop setting that lets eligible users connect several of their own computers and choose which one runs a given dot task. By default, dots still run on dedicated cloud computers with no access to your machine. TokenPost reports the option is for ChatGPT Pro on the $100-plus tiers, Business Premium, and Enterprise workspaces (Enterprise in select markets first), and that the first dot is still included at no extra cost on eligible Pro and Business Premium plans. That narrows one gap with Perplexity Teammates and Claude Duet for work that needs local files or apps. Source: TokenPost (Nov 11, 2026).

Quick verdict

You want an agent that watches team tools (Slack, GitHub, Linear) and fans out work to several sub-agents: Teammates is the most "team ops" of the three, but only if $200 Max is already in your budget. Watch the credit meter: parallel agents, big codebases and frequent automations burn more. You want a personal assistant for your inbox and calendar on the cheapest plan: Claude Duet on Claude Pro ($20, or $17/month billed annually). It's US-only and by waitlist, and it draws on your normal Claude limits, but Pro users now get three full limit resets to try it, usable until Dec 15, 2026. You already pay for ChatGPT Pro for Codex: you already have a dot. Per OpenAI, talking to it doesn't count toward ChatGPT limits; tasks it hands to Codex or Work still do. You're deciding where to put $200 a month: Claude Ultra and ChatGPT Pro 200 each come with an agent and a big coding allowance plus $200 of monthly API credit. Perplexity Max gives you Teammates plus 10,000 Computer credits, and Teammates default to Claude Opus 5.5 anyway. Pick Max only if Perplexity's search and Computer are your main tools. You're outside the US: Duet is US-only. Teammates launched on the web for individual Max without a US-only note in what we read; check during sign-up.

What Perplexity launched on Nov 4

  • What it is: a teammate is a named agent with a standing role. It plans a goal into tasks, runs them in parallel across several agents (each on its own computer), checks the results, and brings finished work back for review: a report, a pull request, or a config change
  • Triggers: your request, a schedule, or new activity in a connected tool. Perplexity's examples include monitoring a Slack alerts channel and replying to each bug report with a likely cause and fix, and watching GitHub, Linear and Datadog to reproduce issues and open a fix PR
  • Where it lives: a Teammates sidebar on the Perplexity web app, built on Perplexity Computer. You can add a teammate to Slack and tag it as an app in Slack, GitHub and Linear. iOS is "coming soon"
  • Models: new teammates default to Claude Opus 5.5; you can set a model per teammate
  • Security model: teammates use your installed plugins and skills with the same permissions; saved credentials are encrypted and hidden from the teammate; each teammate gets an isolated computer with its own browser sessions and cookies
  • Who: research preview for individual Max subscribers. Pro is not included, and Enterprise Pro / Enterprise Max aren't included by default

How Teammates are billed

  • Plan price: Perplexity Max is $200/month or $2,000/year (annual billing on the web only)
  • Credits: per Perplexity's Computer help page, Max includes 10,000 credits a month. Credits are spent only on Computer work (Teammates run on Computer); ordinary Perplexity searches stay unlimited and don't use credits. Consumer Pro gets no monthly credits
  • Launch bonus: the same page lists a limited-time, one-time bonus of 35,000 credits for paid Max subscribers (4,000 for paid Pro), which expire 30 days after they're granted
  • When credits run out: active tasks pause, not cancel, and resume when credits are available. Auto-refill is off until you turn it on; if you do, it buys more when your Max balance drops below 2,500. You can set a monthly spending limit
  • What we don't know: Perplexity doesn't publish how many credits a typical teammate run costs; it says use varies by task complexity. We are not inventing a per-task number

If most of your day is coding, Claude Ultra or ChatGPT Pro 200 give you more for the same money, and both come with an agent. Teammates make sense when the job is ongoing operations across Slack, GitHub and Linear, you want several agents working in parallel, and you already live in Perplexity. Note that a teammate running on Claude Opus 5.5 is paid from Perplexity credits, not your Claude plan. On X over the weekend, OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux asked followers whether a $50 Pro tier would make sense; that's a question, not a product, and we'll update this page if it ships.

Which to pick

  • Founder or ops lead running alerts, bugs and follow-ups through Slack and GitHub: Perplexity Max with Teammates. Turn auto-refill on only with a spending limit, and grant GitHub access to the repos the teammate needs, nothing more
  • Busy professional in the US on a budget: Claude Pro with Duet. Join the waitlist with your Claude email and save the three resets for heavy weeks before Dec 15
  • Heavy coder who also wants an agent: Claude Ultra or ChatGPT Pro 200. Compare in Claude Ultra vs ChatGPT Pro 200
  • Already on ChatGPT Pro: use your dot first before paying for a second $200 plan
  • On Perplexity Pro: you don't get Teammates or monthly credits. The one-time 4,000-credit bonus lets you try Computer, not Teammates
