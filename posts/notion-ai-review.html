Notion AI Review

Update, Oct 20: Notion moved all Notion AI usage to a credit system on Oct 19, 2026. Notion Agent is part of Business ($20/member/month billed yearly, $24 monthly) and Enterprise. Free and Plus get only a trial of Notion AI. Each Business or Enterprise member now gets 1,500 Standard credits (Notion picks the model with Auto) and 500 Select credits (you pick the model) a month, with no rollover. Extra workspace credits cost $10 per 1,000 monthly. New workspaces are on credits now, and existing workspaces switch on Nov 2. You can also connect a ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Go plan so the GPT models you pick in Notion Agent draw on it. What the credits buy, and when ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro is the better spend: Notion AI's new credit system, explained.

Notion AI sits inside the wiki you already live in. That’s the whole pitch — and the limitation.

Quick verdict

Worth it if your source of truth is already Notion and you want summarize / draft / Q&A without exporting. Skip or delay if you mainly need a strong chat model — use ChatGPT/Claude and paste back.
Try Notion AI

What it’s good at

  • Summarizing long pages and meeting notes in-place
  • Drafting from templates you already use
  • Q&A over workspace content (team plans)
  • Keeping AI output next to structured databases

What it’s not

  • A replacement for deep coding assistants
  • Always the strongest raw model vs Plus/Pro chat apps
  • A full automation platform (pair with Make/n8n)

Who should buy

Operators, PMs, and content teams standardized on Notion. Freelancers who only use Notion as a light notes app can often stay on free Notion + an external LLM.
