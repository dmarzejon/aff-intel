Is Anthropic A/B testing Claude Max limits? What's confirmed, how to check your account, and Max vs ChatGPT Pro for heavy coding (2026)

Since Tuesday, a lot of Claude Code users on X have said their usage is draining faster. One post asking "what is going on with Claude Code usage?" passed 237,000 views. On Thursday, Oct 22 (5:36 PM ET), the research firm SemiAnalysis posted that it had "detected an A/B test" on Claude Max. On some accounts, it said, the five-hour limit is about 20% bigger and the monthly value is about 20% smaller. That post passed 210,000 views overnight. Anthropic hasn't confirmed a test. Two Claude Code leads had said earlier that limits hadn't changed. Two hours after the SemiAnalysis post, Boris Cherny wrote: "We're digging into this and will report back very soon." Below: what was measured, what's confirmed, how to check your own account, and what to do if you're paying $100 or $200 a month. We read the posts and Anthropic's and OpenAI's pages early on Oct 23.

Update, Oct 29: Anthropic reported back. On Oct 28 at 7:51 PM ET, Claude Code lead Boris Cherny wrote that the team went through every session ID users shared and "found a few smaller things along the way that made some sessions burn through tokens faster than they should have," with fixes "rolling out right now." He also said: "We didn't find a single root cause or any regression in Claude Code," and that in most cases faster usage "traced back to one or two heavy sessions (xhigh effort, /goal, Workflows, Agent Teams, long SDK jobs)." His tips: watch your effort level, because subagents inherit it by default, and use /usage to see where a session's tokens went. He didn't address the SemiAnalysis A/B test claim directly, so it remains unconfirmed. Separately, ChatGPT Pro now includes $100 to $500 a month in API credits, matching Max's $100/$200. What's confirmed and what isn't

  • Confirmed by Anthropic's own pages: Pro and Max have a five-hour session limit and a weekly limit, shared across claude.ai, the desktop and mobile apps, and Claude Code. Max plans have a separate weekly limit for Fable. Max 5x is $100/month and Max 20x is $200/month (web prices). The Max page also says Anthropic "may limit your usage in other ways, such as weekly and monthly caps or model and feature usage, at our discretion"
  • What Anthropic staff said on X: early Oct 22 (1:34 AM ET), Boris Cherny replied "No changes on our end" and asked for /usage output. That afternoon (4:26 PM ET), Lydia Hallie said "our limits haven't changed" and asked for /feedback IDs. Both replies came before the SemiAnalysis post. At 7:48 PM ET Boris wrote "We're digging into this and will report back very soon," and has been DMing users for details
  • Not confirmed: that a test exists, how many accounts are in it, whether Pro or Max 5x are affected, and whether it's permanent. As of about 4 AM ET on Oct 23 we found no statement on Anthropic's news page, its status page (no incident about limits), or its main X accounts
  • Not a factor here: SemiAnalysis also caught an "extremely tiny" limits test at an unnamed provider in its Oct 5 report. That provider isn't named, so we don't tie it to Anthropic

How to check your own account

  • Settings > Usage on claude.ai: shows your current five-hour session, your weekly limit, your Fable weekly limit (on Max), and your fixed weekly reset day and time
  • In Claude Code: /status shows what's left. Boris asked affected users to run claude -p /usage and share the full output. /feedback in a session that drained fast creates an ID you can send to Anthropic
  • Make it a fair test: compare the same kind of work, on the same model and effort level, a week apart. Opus 5.5 at high effort with lots of subagents burns much faster than Sonnet 5.5, whatever the plan

How to stretch Claude limits this week

  • Watch for stale caches. Lydia Hallie's first question to one user was whether they come back to long sessions "after 1h+ when the prompt cache has expired." Resuming a big session after a long break means re-reading the whole context at full cost. Start a fresh session with a short summary instead
  • Fewer subagents and lower effort for routine work. She also asked whether sessions "use a lot of subagents" and which model and effort level. Save Opus 5.5 at high effort for hard problems; use Sonnet 5.5 for the rest
  • Use Projects for files you reuse. Anthropic's best-practices page says project content is cached and "counts less against your limits when reused"
  • Keep the API key out of the way unless you mean it. If ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is set, Claude Code bills the API instead of your plan. That's useful as a pressure valve, but it costs money at API rates
  • For crunch days, usage credits beat an upgrade. Pro and Max can turn on usage credits to keep working at standard API rates after a limit. The $100 or $200 monthly API credits that come with Max can't be used in Claude Code or the Claude apps
  • Heads-up for next week: starting the week of Oct 26, new sessions in the Claude web, desktop, and mobile apps on Pro, Max, and Team default to auto mode, where an automated check approves safe actions instead of asking you. In Claude Code, Anthropic says it stopped charging Pro, Max, and Team users for the classifier's extra tokens on Aug 14. It hasn't said the same for the apps, so watch your meter for a few days after the switch
