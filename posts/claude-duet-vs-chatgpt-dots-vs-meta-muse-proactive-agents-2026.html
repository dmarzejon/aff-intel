Claude Duet vs ChatGPT dots vs Meta Muse: which proactive AI agent your plan already includes (2026)

On Wednesday, Oct 21 (3:09 PM ET), Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet, a standalone app where Claude works as a proactive agent. It sends you a morning brief, watches your connected apps, takes on bigger jobs in its own cloud computer, and asks before it sends, pays, signs, or deletes anything. Anthropic's launch post on X passed 6 million views in under a day. The pricing hook: Duet is included with Claude Pro and Max at no extra cost during the preview, so it's the cheapest way in to this kind of agent from a big lab. The catch: it runs on your plan's usage limits. A few hours later, OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux posted that ChatGPT's dots are "fully included in the Pro plan, doesn't draw on your weekly usage." But dots need ChatGPT Pro, which starts at $100. Below: what each agent costs, who can get it, how usage works, and which to pick. We read Anthropic's, OpenAI's, and Meta's pages on the morning of Oct 22.

Quick verdict

You're in the US and already pay for Claude Pro ($20, or $17/month annually) or Max: join the Duet waitlist now. It costs nothing extra, and when you're let in you get one full limit reset (usable until Nov 30, 2026). You're on Claude Pro and already hit your limits: expect Duet to make that worse. Its morning brief and background check-ins count against the same 5-hour and weekly limits as chat and Claude Code. You already pay for ChatGPT Pro ($100 / $200 / $500): set up your first dot. Per OpenAI, talking to it doesn't count toward ChatGPT limits, and Tibo says dots don't draw on weekly usage. Tasks it hands to Codex or ChatGPT Work still do. You don't want to pay anything yet: Meta Muse is the only one of the three with a free tier (with a usage limit). Paid Muse is Power $20 or Maximum $100 a month. You're in the EU, UK, or Switzerland: Duet is US-only for now, and dots aren't available to Pro users in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK. Check Muse's availability during onboarding.

What launched on Oct 21

  • What it is: a separate app from the main Claude app, on iOS, Android, and claude.ai on the web. You message it like a person, typed or by voice, in one ongoing thread. It runs on Claude Opus 5.5, and you can't pick a different model
  • What it does: a morning brief from your email and calendar, follow-ups on threads that went quiet, and larger jobs like research briefs, financial models, slide decks, and dashboards. It uses your existing Claude memory, skills, and connectors (Anthropic says over 6,000 connectors and plugins), plus its own cloud browser for sites without a connector
  • Who can get it: Pro and Max subscribers with a US Claude account, through a waitlist. Invitations go out in waves, with no guaranteed date. Team and Enterprise aren't eligible yet; Anthropic says it plans to add them later
  • Price: "During the research preview, Claude Duet is included with the Pro and Max plans at no extra cost"
  • Usage: it uses your plan's 5-hour session and weekly limits "at the same rate as chats in Claude." Background work like the morning brief and check-ins also counts, so Anthropic says "you may reach your limits sooner than with chat alone." To slow the drain, it checks your accounts every few hours instead of constantly
  • The one-time reset: when you're admitted, you get one full limit reset (both the 5-hour and weekly limits), available until Nov 30, 2026
  • Payments and approvals: it asks before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document, or deletes anything. Purchases go through Stripe's Link or Shopify's Shop Pay with a one-time card per purchase. Some actions are off-limits even with your approval, like trading securities or moving money between accounts
  • Not ready yet: you can't watch or take over its browser, it doesn't call or text other people, Slack works with limits, and Microsoft 365 isn't available yet. Anthropic says it can misread a thread or misjudge what's urgent

The real cost: limits, not dollars

  • Duet shares one pool with everything else. On Claude, chat, Claude Code, and now Duet all draw from the same 5-hour and weekly limits. If you use Claude Code heavily on Pro, a proactive agent checking your inbox every few hours will eat into that. Anthropic says bigger jobs like a financial model or a deck "require more model turns and background work"
  • Dots are metered differently. OpenAI's launch post says conversations with your dot don't count toward ChatGPT usage limits, and the first month came with extended limits for deeper work. On Oct 21 Tibo added that dots are "fully included in the Pro plan, doesn't draw on your weekly usage." Anything your dot launches in Codex or ChatGPT Work still counts toward those limits as usual
  • The first-month window for dots: dots launched Sep 29. OpenAI says "first month after launch" but hasn't given an end date or the terms that come after. If it's counted from launch day, it would end around Oct 29; that date is our inference, not OpenAI's
  • Muse is the only one with published allowance numbers: 500M Muse tokens a week on Power and 3B on Maximum. It's the easiest to budget, but Muse tokens aren't comparable to Claude or ChatGPT limits

Which to pick

  • Small business owner or busy professional in the US, on a budget: Claude Pro at $20 is the cheapest paid plan with a frontier-lab proactive agent included. Join the Duet waitlist with the same email as your Claude account, since access is tied to that address
  • Heavy Claude Code user: either keep Duet's scope narrow (tell it what to watch and how often), or plan on Max. Save the one-time reset for a crunch week before Nov 30
  • Already on ChatGPT Pro for Codex: you've already paid for a dot. Use it for ongoing projects, and keep in mind that the Codex tasks it starts still draw on your Codex limits
  • Want to try a personal agent for $0: Muse Free. Connect your calendar before your email or payments
  • Outside the US: Duet isn't available yet. Dots on ChatGPT Pro work in markets outside the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK, and Business Premium lists all supported ChatGPT regions
