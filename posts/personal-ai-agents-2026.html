Personal AI agents compared: Muse, Sesame, Claude Cowork, Gemini Spark

Chatbots answer. Personal agents act — email, calendar, bookings, sometimes purchases. Mid-September 2026 is a launch cluster: Meta Muse (Sep 8, US), Sesame’s conversational iOS preview (public this week), plus Claude Cowork and Gemini Spark already in paid ecosystems. Here’s how to pick without handing every account to the wrong vendor.

Quick verdict

Want real-world actions (book / buy / inbox) and you’re in the US: try Meta Muse on Free first — connect calendar before email or payments. Want natural voice conversation without giving bank access: Sesame iOS (free preview; waitlist possible). Want agent work inside tools you already pay for: Claude Cowork (with paid Claude) or Gemini Spark (with Google AI Pro) — lower trust jump if you already live in those stacks. Need coding / shipping agents instead: stay on Cursor + Claude — different category than Muse/Sesame.

How to adopt without over-trusting

  1. Start narrow: calendar + restaurants before Gmail + payments.
  2. Prefer free tiers for week-1 experiments — Muse Free and Sesame preview exist specifically to let you feel the product.
  3. Separate life agents from coding agents — don’t expect Muse to replace Cursor Projects, or Sesame to refactor a monorepo.
  4. Watch privacy claims carefully — “we won’t look” ≠ “we can’t look.” Muse’s Confidential VM is still roadmap.
