At 12:54 PM ET on Tuesday, Nov 17, 2026, OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra (gpt-6.2-astra), its new frontier model "for your hardest projects", and GPT-6.2 Sol (gpt-6.2-sol), its everyday model. OpenAI's launch post passed 7.2 million views and 33,000 likes by early Nov 18, the day Anthropic prices its IPO. The questions in the replies: what does it cost, which ChatGPT plan gets it, can you use it in Copilot or Cursor, and does it beat Claude Fable 5.2? We read OpenAI's announcement, API model and pricing pages, the ChatGPT Learn release note and pricing page, OpenAI's Help Center, GitHub's changelog, OpenRouter's listing and Artificial Analysis's model pages on the morning of Nov 18.

Quick verdict

You pay per token for a top model: GPT-6.2 Astra lists at $8 in / $40 out per million tokens ($0.40 cached), exactly Claude Fable 5.2's list price and 20% below GPT-6 Astra ($10/$50). Per finished task it's far cheaper than Fable: Artificial Analysis measured $4.00 and 11.5 minutes per Intelligence Index task for a score of 62, against $26.54 and 41 minutes for Fable 5.2's 65. You want the best value: GPT-6.2 Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's $2/$10 and scores 57 for $1.01 per task, the same score as GPT-6 Astra at about a sixth of its per-task cost. For most API work, start with Sol. You want the absolute top score: Fable 5.2 still leads Artificial Analysis (65, #1 of 92). Astra is #3. If you have Claude Max or Ultra, Fable is included up to half your weekly limit. You're on ChatGPT Plus ($20): both models are included, but only in ChatGPT Work and Codex, not regular Chat. OpenAI estimates 6–50 Astra or 17–180 Sol local messages per five-hour window on Plus. Pro tiers have no five-hour limit. You code in an editor: it's in GitHub Copilot (Pro+, Max, Business, Enterprise) at API list price. It's not in Cursor: OpenAI isn't giving Cursor new models, and Cursor's access to OpenAI's older models currently runs through Nov 25.

What launched

  • GPT-6.2 Astra: OpenAI's new top model for complex professional work, coding, science and "slides, spreadsheets, and documents". Text and image in, text out; 1,050,000-token context (922K max input), 128K max output, May 18, 2026 knowledge cutoff. OpenAI says it scores 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam "at less than a third of the cost" (vendor benchmark)
  • GPT-6.2 Sol: the everyday model. Same 1.05M context and 128K output, Jun 1, 2026 cutoff. OpenAI says it comes within 0.5 points of Astra on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 at about a fifth of the cost (vendor-reported)
  • Where: ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu (Enterprise/Edu admins must enable it); the OpenAI API (Chat Completions, Responses, Batch); GitHub Copilot; OpenRouter (openai/gpt-6.2-astra via OpenAI, Azure and Amazon Bedrock at the same $8/$40 list)
  • Not where: regular ChatGPT Chat (OpenAI's Help Center: "This rollout does not add either model to regular Chat") and Cursor
  • Safety: OpenAI rates both models Critical for cybersecurity and High for bio/chem under its Preparedness Framework, and calls Astra its first released model at High for AI self-improvement. They ship with tighter cyber safeguards, which OpenAI says "also restrict some legitimate vulnerability research and security testing workflows"

What a request costs (list prices)

One call with 100K input and 10K output tokens, no caching: GPT-6.2 Astra $1.20 · Claude Fable 5.2 $1.20 · Claude Opus 5.5 $0.60 · GPT-6.2 Sol $0.30 · Gemini 4 Carbon $0.30 now, $0.60 from Dec 1 · old GPT-6 Astra $1.50. Same per call for Astra and Fable, but on Artificial Analysis's runs Fable wrote about four times as many output tokens (275M vs 65.8M), which is why its per-task cost is more than six times higher. Your mix will differ; test on your own prompts.

Caching matters more than usual: Astra's cached input is $0.40, 60% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra's, and OpenRouter shows the average effective input price customers actually paid on day one at about $1.35–$1.45 per million on the main endpoints because of high cache-hit rates. OpenAI recommends keeping prompt prefixes unchanged and appending new instructions to get those hits.

ChatGPT plans: Plus vs Pro vs Business

  • Plus ($20/month): Astra and Sol in Work and Codex. OpenAI's estimates per five-hour window: Astra 6–50, Sol 17–180 local messages (GPT-6 Astra was 5–45, GPT-6.1 Sol 15–160). Weekly limits may also apply. New Work chats default to Sol at Light reasoning effort
  • Pro 100 / Pro 200 / Pro 500: no five-hour limit in Work and Codex (an included allowance still applies). Ultrafast comes to Pro 200 for the first time, with Sol only; Pro 500 gets Ultrafast with both models
  • Business Standard: same estimates as Plus. Enterprise/Edu get access once an admin enables it; Enterprise defaults to Sol at Medium
  • Out of allowance: Plus and Pro can buy credits. Astra costs 200 credits per million input tokens, 10 cached and 1,000 output; Sol 50 / 2.5 / 250, per ChatGPT's pricing page. Or run extra local chats with an API key at API rates
  • Coming in December: OpenAI says Plus, Pro and Business usage will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex and measured in tokens, as Work's agent features move into main ChatGPT

Coding tools: Codex, Copilot, Cursor

  • Codex (app, CLI, IDE extension): both models from day one on Plus and up; switch with the model picker or /model. OpenAI pitches Astra for tracing bugs across services and Sol for PR review and revisions
  • GitHub Copilot: generally available Nov 17 for Copilot Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise (not plain Pro), rolling out gradually, billed at provider list pricing under usage-based billing. In VS Code, Visual Studio, JetBrains, Xcode, Eclipse, Copilot CLI, the coding agent and github.com. GitHub says Sol caught "nearly twice as many real issues in code review" as GPT-6.1 Sol in early testing
  • Cursor: not available. OpenAI said in August it would wind down its contract with Cursor after SpaceX bought it and is "not providing future models to Cursor". Cursor's own model list tops out at GPT-5.6. Cursor staff said Nov 16 that OpenAI extended access to its existing models "through November 25". Bring-your-own OpenAI key or the Codex extension inside Cursor are the workarounds OpenAI documents; a BYO key covers Cursor's local Chat and Agent, not Tab or cloud agents, per third-party guides

Which to pick

  • API, most workloads: GPT-6.2 Sol. Same price as 6.1 Sol, better scores, and the best per-task cost in this table. Compare with Gemini 4 Hydrogen ($1.50/$7.50, 51 at $1.87 per task) if price matters more than score; see our Hydrogen guide
  • API, hardest tasks on a budget: GPT-6.2 Astra. Within 3 points of Fable 5.2 on Artificial Analysis at about 15% of its per-task cost, and 3.5x faster per task
  • Maximum quality, cost secondary, or you already pay for Claude Max/Ultra: Claude Fable 5.2. See our Fable 5.2 plan guide
  • Google shop: Gemini 4 Carbon is cheap until Nov 30, then doubles; on Artificial Analysis it's slower and pricier per task than Astra even at the intro price. See our Carbon guide
  • Subscription, light use: ChatGPT Plus covers both in Codex and Work. Heavy agent users should look at Pro tiers (no five-hour cap); see our Pro 100 vs Pro 500 guide
  • Cursor user who wants GPT-6.2: install the Codex extension or add an API key before Nov 25, or move OpenAI work to Copilot Pro+
  • Security researchers: expect more refusals on 6.2; OpenAI points defenders to its Daybreak program, where 6.2 isn't available yet
