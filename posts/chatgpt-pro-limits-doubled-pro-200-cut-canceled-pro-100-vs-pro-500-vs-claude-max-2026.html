OpenAI doubled ChatGPT Pro limits and canceled the Pro 200 cut: Pro 100 vs Pro 200 vs Pro 500 vs Claude Max, now (2026)

One day before grandfathered Pro 200 subscribers were due to drop from 20x to 10x the Plus allowance, OpenAI reversed course. On Oct 29 (12:56 PM ET), Codex lead Tibo Sottiaux posted: "We are doubling all usage limits for all Pro 100/200/500 tiers and Business Premium, effective today. We heard your kind feedback and won't be reducing usage limits for Pro 200." OpenAI's Help Center confirms it: Pro 100 and Pro 500 now include twice their previous Work and Codex allowance, the Pro 200 reduction is canceled, and Pro 200 subscribers who were on the lower allowance doubled to match. Prices don't change. The post has 4.4M views, and it's the top AI story on X this morning. We read OpenAI's Help Center pages and release notes on Oct 30.

Quick verdict

You're a grandfathered Pro 200 user who was about to downgrade or leave: stay. Nothing changes for you, and your GPT-6 Pro messages in Chat stay where they were. You joined Pro 200 after DevDay (Sep 29) on the lower allowance: you just doubled, automatically. No need to cancel and resubscribe. You're on Pro 100: you now get about 10x Plus for $100 (it was 5x). If you were thinking of moving up to Pro 200, check your meter for a week first; you may not need to. You're on Pro 500: about 50x Plus now. Ultrafast still burns it at 8x, so the doubling mostly buys you more Ultrafast time. You're choosing between ChatGPT Pro 100 and Claude Max 5x at $100: on paper, OpenAI now gives twice the multiple of its entry plan. Multiples aren't comparable across vendors, so decide by which coding agent and models you prefer, then check your meter.

What changed on Oct 29

  • Pro 100 and Pro 500: "now include twice their previous Work and Codex allowance." New subscriptions get it, and existing subscribers get it "automatically without waiting for renewal"
  • Pro 200: "The reduction announced in our September 29 email is canceled. If you already had the higher Work and Codex allowance, you keep it. If you had the lower allowance, it has doubled to match." So every Pro 200 subscription now has the same allowance, and new Pro 200 sign-ups are open again at $200
  • Business Premium: Tibo's post and OpenAI's Business release notes include Business Premium seats in the increase. OpenAI hasn't published a new Premium multiple
  • GPT-6 Pro in Chat: Pro 100 and Pro 500 keep their current weekly GPT-6 Pro message limits. On Pro 200, "the lower weekly limit now matches the higher one." These are separate from the Work and Codex allowance
  • Dots: OpenAI will "continue offering extended limits for dots for now," and dot usage stays separate from your default usage
  • Unchanged: Plus usage, prices, Fast mode (2.5x the Standard rate), Ultrafast on Pro 500 (8x included, 6x when paying in credits), and no five-hour limit on Pro. OpenAI also warns that "a larger allowance does not guarantee twice as many completed tasks"
  • The $2,500 credit grant: OpenAI's updated pages don't mention the one-time 62,500-credit grant from the Sep 29 email. Several Pro 200 subscribers on X say they still have it. Check Settings > Usage before you count on it; if it's there, it still expires Dec 31

Which plan now

  • Light Codex or Work user on Plus who keeps hitting the limit: Pro 100 is now 10x for 5x the price. That's the best jump in the lineup
  • Pro 200 user who used under half the meter: try Pro 100 for a month. At 10x it should cover the same week, and you'd save $100. Upgrading back is instant
  • Heavy Codex user: Pro 200 at 20x is back to the deal it was before DevDay. Pro 500 only makes sense if you want Ultrafast or regularly run out of 20x
  • Considering a move to Claude Max: the reason most people gave this week was the Pro 200 cut, and that's gone. Switch only if you prefer Claude Code or Opus 5.5 for the work itself. See Claude Max limits vs ChatGPT Pro
