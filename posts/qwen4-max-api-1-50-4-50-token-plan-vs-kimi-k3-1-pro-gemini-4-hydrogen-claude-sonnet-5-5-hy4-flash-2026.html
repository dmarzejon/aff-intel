Qwen4-Max is out: $1.50/$4.50 API, Token Plan, no open weights. Is it a better buy than Kimi K3.1 Pro, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Claude Sonnet 5.5 or Tencent's Hy4 flash? (Nov 2026)

On the evening of Sunday, Nov 15, 2026 (US time; Nov 16 in China), Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen4-Max (qwen4-max), the first model in the Qwen4 series: a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 71 billion active parameters, native image and video input, and a 1M-token context window. @Alibaba_Qwen's launch post passed 2.2 million views and 14,000 likes by early Nov 17. Unlike most earlier Qwen releases, the weights are closed: it's API and app only. The same day Tencent launched Hy4 flash at a tiny fraction of the price. The questions in the replies: what does Qwen4-Max cost, can you still self-host it, and is it actually a better deal than Kimi K3.1 Pro, Gemini 4 Hydrogen or Claude? We read Alibaba's announcement, the QwenCloud model page, pricing docs and Token Plan docs, OpenRouter's listing, Artificial Analysis's model and comparison pages, Anthropic's pricing page, Tencent's Hy4 flash announcement, and coverage from DataCamp, The New Stack and The Next Web on the morning of Nov 17.

Update, Nov 18: Tencent has published open weights for Hy4 flash: the instruct model, an FP8 version and the pre-trained base, on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode, with serving code on GitHub. The license is the Tencent Hy License 1.0: derived from Apache 2.0 but not Apache 2.0, with a Section 10 that requires a separate written license from Tencent for model-as-a-service businesses above $50 million in annual worldwide revenue or products with more than 100 million monthly active users. Qwen4-Max is still closed, and Alibaba still hasn't dated its smaller open Qwen4 models. Separately, OpenAI's new GPT-6.2 Sol ($2/$10) scores 57 on Artificial Analysis for $1.01 per task; see our GPT-6.2 guide.

Quick verdict

You want the cheapest per-token frontier-ish model with a 1M context: Qwen4-Max's list price ($1.50 in / $4.50 out per million tokens, $0.075 cached) undercuts Claude Sonnet 5.5 ($2/$10), Kimi K3.1 Pro ($2/$10) and Gemini 4 Hydrogen on output ($7.50). It's a reasonable pick for long-document and office-style agent work, which is where Alibaba's own benchmarks look best. You care about cost per finished task, not per token: Qwen4-Max is very verbose and slow. Artificial Analysis measured $3.27 and 32 minutes per Intelligence Index task for a score of 43. Gemini 4 Hydrogen at its default (high) setting scored 51 for $1.87 and about 8 minutes. Kimi K3.1 Pro scored 46 for $3.68. On that benchmark, Hydrogen is the better buy. You code in a terminal agent: skip it for now. Qwen4-Max scores 48.5% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against 67.2% for Kimi K3.1 Pro (Artificial Analysis runs both); Alibaba's own chart has Claude Opus 5.5 at 59.6%. You wanted open weights: Qwen4-Max has none, and Alibaba hasn't promised any. It says smaller Qwen4 models will be open-weight "soon", with no size or date. Kimi K3.1 Pro is the big open model today. You just need cheap, fast bulk work: Tencent's Hy4 flash lists at $0.07/$0.281, about 1/20th of Qwen4-Max's input price, and is half price on TokenHub through Nov 30.

What launched

  • Qwen4-Max (Alibaba, Nov 16 China time): 2.4T total / 71B active parameters, "25% fewer activated parameters" than Qwen3.8-Max at the same scale. Text, image and video in, text out. QwenCloud lists a 1M context, up to 131K output (262K with thinking), tool calling, structured outputs, batches, context cache, built-in web search, and OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs
  • Where: the QwenCloud API and Token Plan, plus Alibaba's QwenWork and Qwen Studio apps. Third parties had it on day one, including OpenRouter (qwen/qwen4-max), Vercel's AI Gateway and Novita, per their posts
  • Weights: closed. Alibaba: "Open-weight, smaller-scale models from the Qwen4 series will soon be released." No sizes or dates. As of Nov 16, third-party "Qwen4-27B" repos on Hugging Face are empty shells, per Ask This Guy; download nothing that isn't from the official Qwen org
  • Alibaba's efficiency claim: on its in-house Work360 benchmark, Qwen4-Max beats Qwen3.8-Max using about 24% (xhigh) to 35% (high) fewer tokens, and average cost per task drops from $4.43 to $1.67. That's a vendor benchmark
  • Hy4 flash (Tencent, Nov 16): 224B total / 12B active, multimodal input, 1M context, about $0.070 in / $0.281 out / $0.003 cached per million tokens. In Tencent's WorkBuddy, Yuanbao and TokenHub; OpenRouter "coming soon"; Tencent says it plans to open-source it

What a request costs (list prices)

One call with 100K input tokens and 10K output tokens, no caching: Qwen4-Max $0.195 · Gemini 4 Hydrogen $0.225 · Claude Sonnet 5.5 $0.30 · Kimi K3.1 Pro $0.30 · Claude Opus 5.5 $0.60 · Hy4 flash about $0.01. The catch is the 10K: Artificial Analysis says Qwen4-Max produced 127 million output tokens to finish its index, against a median of 78.6 million for models in its class. Reasoning tokens count as output, so a cheaper rate doesn't guarantee a cheaper task. Test on your own prompts before switching.

Benchmarks: where it's strong and where it isn't

  • Strong (third-party): #6 on Arena's Code Arena WebDev leaderboard (1,750, preliminary) and #6 in Image-to-WebDev; top Chinese model on the Vals Index (#11 overall). On Artificial Analysis it edges Kimi K3.1 Pro on finance and legal, clearly beats it on HLE-Diamond (44.9% vs 37.3%), and outputs faster (54 vs 31 tokens/second)
  • Strong (Alibaba's own): 75.7 on DeepSWE v1.1 vs 74.2 for Opus 5.5; 46.7 on Agents' Last Exam vs 43.4 for Opus 5.5, per DataCamp's read of the launch chart
  • Weak: terminal coding (48.5% Terminal-Bench 4.0, confirmed by Artificial Analysis and Vals), science tasks (23.3% Terminal-Bench-Science vs 34.3% for Kimi), speed (Artificial Analysis ranks it 74th of 79 on performance, with a 148-second wait for the first answer token), and verbosity
  • Overall: Artificial Analysis puts it at 43, #38 of 81 models, "above average" for its price class (median 41) but well behind the top US models

Token Plan: the subscription route

  • QwenCloud's Token Plan Individual includes qwen4-max. Plans: Lite $6/month (20,000 credits; $3 limited-time new-user price), Essential $10 (45,000; $5 new-user), Standard $18 (80,000), Pro $43 (200,000). Credit packs are $15 for 20,000 and need an active plan
  • When credits run out and you have no pack, service pauses until the next subscription month. We didn't find a published credits-per-token rate for qwen4-max on the overview page, so we can't say how far 20,000 credits go
  • qwen4-max-preview retires Nov 25 (midnight UTC+8, which is 11 AM ET). If you built against the preview ID during the Nov 11–25 promo, switch to qwen4-max
  • QwenCloud's pricing docs list a 1 million-token free quota for qwen4-max, Singapore region only

Which to pick

  • Long documents, slides, spreadsheets, office agents on a budget: try Qwen4-Max through OpenRouter first (no new account; one provider, Alibaba Cloud International, and no EU in-region routing yet)
  • Best intelligence per dollar in this price band: Gemini 4 Hydrogen at its default setting. Same $1.50 input, higher output price, but fewer tokens and much faster on Artificial Analysis's runs. See our Hydrogen guide
  • Agentic coding: Kimi K3.1 Pro or Claude. Kimi leads Qwen4-Max by almost 19 points on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and has open weights. See our Kimi K3.1 Pro open-weights guide
  • Self-hosting: not an option with Qwen4-Max. Wait for the smaller Qwen4 open models, or use Kimi K3.1 Pro
  • High-volume, simple tasks: Hy4 flash is the price outlier, but it's on Tencent's platforms until it reaches OpenRouter, and its quality numbers are Tencent's own
  • Data residency: check QwenCloud's region list before sending sensitive data. Models and prices differ by region and deployment scope
