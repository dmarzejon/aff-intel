Beehiiv raised its prices: what Lite and Pro will cost you at renewal after Nov 1, and when Kit, Ghost, MailCheap, or Substack is cheaper (2026)

On Oct 1, 2026, beehiiv launched new plans and pricing. Launch is now Free, Scale is now Lite, and Max is now Pro. New customers pay the new prices now. Existing customers keep their current plan and price until their first renewal on or after Nov 1, 2026. Monthly customers get at least 30 days' notice before the first bill at the new price, so those notices are landing now. Annual customers keep their price until the end of the current term. The Verge's example: at 10,000 subscribers, Lite costs $139/month instead of $109, and Pro costs $239/month instead of $219. On Oct 15, indie founder Marc Lou launched MailCheap, an open-source newsletter tool that charges per email instead of per subscriber. His launch post passed 430,000 views on X, and his earlier "Bye bye Beehiiv" post passed 394,000. The replies are full of creators running the math for their own list. Below is that math, done carefully. We read beehiiv's Oct 1 announcement and pricing page, The Verge's report, the Kit, Ghost(Pro), and MailCheap pricing pages, and MailCheap's GitHub repo on Oct 15–16.

Quick verdict

You're under 2,500 subscribers: nothing to do. beehiiv's Free plan still covers up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends, and now includes community, podcasts, and digital products. Kit's Free plan goes up to 10,000 subscribers if you outgrow that and don't need automations. You earn from beehiiv's Ad Network, paid subs, or recommendations: staying is often right. beehiiv still takes 0% of paid subscriptions (Substack takes 10%). Switch to annual billing, which beehiiv says is now up to two months free, and clean out inactive subscribers before your renewal date. You run automations, tags, and digital-product funnels: price Kit. Creator is $33/month and Pro is $66/month billed yearly at 1,000 subscribers. Pro is currently discounted to $50/month for the first year, and Kit offers free migration. You want a website + newsletter + paid memberships with 0% platform fees: Ghost(Pro) Publisher is $29/month billed yearly at 1,000 members. You send one issue a week to a big list and don't use beehiiv's monetization: MailCheap can be much cheaper, from $19/month. But it's brand new, has no free plan or trial, and gets more expensive if you send daily (see the math below).

What changed at beehiiv (Oct 1, 2026)

  • Plan names: Launch → Free, Scale → Lite, Max → Pro. Enterprise stays Enterprise (custom pricing, now at any list size)
  • Who pays what, when: new customers pay the new prices from Oct 1. Existing customers move at their first renewal on or after Nov 1, 2026. Monthly plans get at least 30 days' notice. Annual plans keep their price through the end of the current term, then move over automatically
  • Price at 10,000 subscribers, monthly billing (The Verge): Lite $109 → $139, Pro $219 → $239
  • Bigger lists take the biggest jump. Behind the Change compared archived and current pricing (billed annually, per month). At 1,000 subscribers, Lite went from $43 to $49, and Pro was roughly flat ($96 to $95). At 100,000 subscribers, Lite went from $290 to $399 (+38%), and Pro from $404 to $599 (+48%)
  • What you get for it: Pro now covers up to 250,000 subscribers without a sales call (self-serve plans used to stop at 100,000). beehiiv also says there's more AI on every plan, every core product (including podcasts, community, MCP, and digital products) on every plan including Free, and a bigger annual discount
  • Limits that went down: per Behind the Change, Lite has 2 team seats where Scale had 3, and Pro has 10 where Max had unlimited
  • Check your tier: beehiiv says it moved some accounts that had outgrown their billed tier to the tier that matches their current subscriber count. That's also billed at your first renewal on or after Nov 1
  • Discounts carry over. Your existing discount moves to the new plan. Discounts can't be combined, so a new offer later (like Black Friday) replaces it

Is MailCheap really "83% cheaper"? Only if you send weekly

MailCheap's headline example is 4,000 subscribers and one email a week: $19 on MailCheap Starter vs $109 on beehiiv Lite (monthly billing). That's accurate, and MailCheap's own table also shows $39 vs $139 at 10,000 and $149 vs $299 at 50,000, both at weekly sending. But MailCheap charges per email, so how often you send matters as much as list size. Several creators pointed this out in replies to the launch. Our math below uses each company's published prices and about 30 sends a month for a daily newsletter:

  • 5,000 subscribers, daily: about 150,000 emails a month → MailCheap Pro $79, vs beehiiv Lite $109 monthly or $95/month annual. MailCheap is still cheaper, but by less
  • 10,000 subscribers, daily: about 300,000 emails a month → MailCheap Scale $149, vs beehiiv Lite $139 monthly or $119/month annual. beehiiv is cheaper
  • 4,000 subscribers, weekly: about 17,000 emails → MailCheap Starter $19 vs beehiiv Lite $109. This is MailCheap's best case
  • What MailCheap doesn't replace: MailCheap's own comparison page says "beehiiv has more ways to grow and earn." If the Ad Network, paid recommendations, or paid subs are paying for your plan, price the lost revenue too
  • Caveats: it launched Oct 15, so there's no track record yet. There's no free plan or trial. And sends pause when you hit your monthly email cap, until you upgrade or the month resets. Skeptics on X raised deliverability and sender-reputation questions, which only time will answer. If you try it, warm up your own sending domain and keep your beehiiv export

Five ways to cut your beehiiv bill before your renewal date

  1. Find your renewal date. If it's on or after Nov 1, that's your first bill at the new price. Monthly customers get 30 days' notice. Annual customers keep the old price until their term ends.
  2. Clean your list. beehiiv bills by active subscribers. Removing people who haven't opened in months can drop you a tier, and beehiiv suggests this itself. At 10,000 vs 5,000 on Lite, that's $139 vs $109 a month.
  3. Go annual if you're staying. At 10,000 subscribers, Lite is $119/month billed annually vs $139 monthly. beehiiv says annual is now up to two months free.
  4. Downgrade Pro to Lite if you don't use branding removal, more than 3 publications, more than 2 seats, dynamic content, or the Send API. At 10,000 subscribers that's $239 → $139 a month.
  5. Price one alternative on your real numbers (list size × sends per month, plus any beehiiv earnings you'd lose) before your renewal date. Kit and Ghost both have free or low-cost entry points, and Kit migrates for free.
