Claude is now inside Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides: how it compares with Gemini, and whether Claude Pro or Google AI Pro is the better $20 for your files (2026)

On Oct 6, 2026, Anthropic opened Claude for Google Workspace as a public beta on all paid Claude plans (Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise). One install from the Google Workspace Marketplace adds a Claude sidebar to Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Claude reads the file you have open, sees what you’ve selected, and edits it in place. Anthropic’s launch post on X passed 13 million views. That puts Claude in the same spot as Google’s own Gemini, which already lives in those apps. The catch: free Claude doesn’t get the add-on, and on a personal Google account Gemini in Docs and Sheets needs a paid Google AI plan too. So either way you’re paying about $20. We read Anthropic’s announcement, its Help Center guide, its pricing page, and Google’s plan pages on the morning of Oct 12.

Quick verdict

You already pay for Claude Pro or Max: install the add-on today. It costs nothing extra, one install covers Docs, Sheets, and Slides, and your Claude models, connectors, and skills come with you. You live in Sheets (models, formulas, messy data): this is where Claude’s add-on is strongest. It writes and fixes formulas, builds pivot tables and native charts, adds tabs, and can pull a range into Python for joins and cleanup, then write the results back. You want AI in Docs and Sheets plus Google storage and perks: Google AI Pro ($19.99/month) bundles Gemini in Google apps with 5 TB of storage and other Google One extras. If you’d pay for the storage anyway, it’s the better value. You use Firefox, or need Docs comments handled: wait. In this beta Claude’s actions don’t complete in Firefox, and it can’t read, reply to, or resolve Docs comments. You’re on free Claude and free Gmail: neither assistant works inside your Docs for free. Pick the one whose chat app you already prefer, and pay for that.

What Anthropic actually shipped (Oct 6)

  • Who gets it: public beta on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise. Not on free Claude. You also need a Google account that’s allowed to install Marketplace apps. Work accounts may need an admin to allowlist or install it
  • Install: Google Workspace Marketplace → Install, then open any file and go to Extensions → Claude → Open Claude. One install covers all three apps. Admins can deploy it to a whole domain or chosen groups
  • Docs: fixes sentences and restyles headings in place, rewrites and restructures long documents, and can propose bigger edits as suggestion cards you apply or dismiss
  • Sheets: writes and fixes formulas, builds pivot tables and native Sheets charts, adds tabs, and uses Python for joins and data cleaning
  • Slides: builds new slides from your deck’s own layouts and themes, then checks for overlapping elements, text running off the slide, and hard-to-read text
  • Two modes: “Ask before edits” (the default) shows each change for approval. “Accept all edits” works without stopping, but still pauses for links, images from the web, and anything that changes who can access the file. Every edit lands in version history under your name
  • Your Claude setup comes along: the sidebar uses the same models, connectors, and skills as your Claude account
  • Separate beta, other direction: new Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides connectors let you start in Claude and have it create or edit Google files from the chat. Access follows your Drive sharing permissions. On Team and Enterprise, an owner has to turn them on first
  • Enterprise: Compliance API, customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK), and OpenTelemetry audit export apply to the add-on. Not supported in this beta: third-party inference (Bedrock, Vertex AI, Azure), zero data retention, and HIPAA use

Beta limits worth knowing before you pay

  • Browsers: Chrome, Edge, or Safari. In Firefox the sidebar loads, but actions don’t complete
  • One file at a time: Claude only sees and edits the file it’s opened in, unless you turn on the Google Drive connector. It can’t export to PDF
  • Keep the sidebar open: closing it stops the task. A single long operation can run up to six minutes before Google stops it, so split big jobs
  • Docs: no reading or resolving comments, and Claude’s edits land as direct edits, not tracked suggestions
  • Sheets: Connected Sheets (live BigQuery) tabs must be extracted to a regular tab first. No triggers, custom menus, or scheduled refreshes
  • Slides: charts are inserted as images, not linked Sheets charts, so they don’t update when the numbers change
  • Chat history is saved per file in your browser and doesn’t sync across devices

Checklist before you pick

  1. Already on Claude Pro or Max? Install it from the Marketplace and run one real task in Sheets, such as “walk me through how this total is calculated and flag anything hardcoded.” It costs you nothing extra.
  2. Check your browser. Firefox users will see the sidebar but nothing will happen. Use Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
  3. Work account? If Install is greyed out or you see “not allowed by your administrator,” that’s Google’s setting. Only your Workspace admin can allowlist Claude.
  4. Start in “Ask before edits.” Switch to “Accept all edits” only after you trust it on that file. Version history can undo anything.
  5. Price the bundle. Google AI Pro includes 5 TB of storage and other Google One perks. If you already pay for Google storage, Gemini is the cheaper path. If you don’t, compare Claude Pro and AI Pro on the job itself.
  6. Regulated data? Don’t use the beta add-on with health data. It isn’t HIPAA-ready and has no zero-retention option yet.
