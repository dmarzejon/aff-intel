Claude Personal Finance vs ChatGPT Finances: Pro/Max/Ultra plan gate, hub privacy, credit score, and what each actually reads (Nov 2026)

On Nov 5, 2026, Anthropic opened a beta of Personal Finance in Claude: connect US bank and card accounts through Plaid, then ask about balances and spending in plain language. Access is read-only — it can't move, manage, or invest your money. The catch for buyers: it rolls out only on Pro, Max, and Ultra in the US (not Free, Team, or Enterprise), and the rollout is gradual. ChatGPT already has Finances (OpenAI launched the connected-accounts path in May 2026); OpenAI's help center says it is available in the US on Free, Go, Plus, and Pro, with Plaid for accounts and a separate Experian path for credit score and report. We read Anthropic's launch post and Help Center article, Plaid's partner post, and OpenAI's Finances help page on the morning of Nov 6. Below: what each product can see, how training and ads are described, and which plan (if any) you need before you connect a bank.

Quick verdict

You already pay for Claude Pro ($20/month, or $17/month billed annually), Max ($100), or Ultra ($200) in the US: try Personal Finance when it appears in the sidebar. Hub chats are not used to train Anthropic's models, Anthropic says there are no ads or sponsored product pitches, and setup is Plaid-only. You want bank + spending help without paying: ChatGPT Finances on Free (US) is the open door. Claude Personal Finance is paid-plan only. You care about credit score and report factors: ChatGPT Finances can connect Experian (VantageScore 3.0). Claude's Help Center does not offer a credit-bureau link; it reads balances and credit limits from linked cards/loans, not a bureau score. You care about brokerage holdings and allocation: ChatGPT's dashboard includes portfolio distribution and equity market updates when the institution shares that data. Claude today reads balances and credit limits for investments/loans/cards — not holdings, trades, rates, or terms. You want the stricter training default for money chats: Claude's Personal Finance hub chats are excluded from training by product design. ChatGPT Finances follows the same Data controls setting as the rest of ChatGPT — change it in Settings if you don't want training.

What launched on Nov 5 (Claude)

  • Product: a Personal Finance hub in Claude's sidebar with widgets (spending, cash flow, accounts, subscriptions, upcoming bills, short insights) plus questions in the hub or in regular chat
  • Who: beta on Pro, Max, and Ultra plans in the US only. Not on Free, Team, or Enterprise. Gradual rollout — you may not see it yet
  • Where: Claude on the web, Claude Desktop, Claude for iOS, and Claude for Android
  • Connector: Plaid. You sign in at your bank through Plaid; Claude does not ask for or see your bank password
  • Read-only: cannot move, manage, or invest money. Not designed to give investment advice. Anthropic and the Help Center both say Claude can make mistakes — check important figures with your bank
  • No ads / no Claude financial products: Anthropic's launch post says Personal Finance has no ads or sponsored recommendations, and Claude doesn't offer its own credit cards, loans, or savings accounts
  • Bank limits: up to 16 banks; US banks only

What ChatGPT Finances already does

  • Who: US users on Free, Go, Plus, and Pro, on web, iOS, and Android (OpenAI Help Center)
  • Two separate connections: financial accounts through Plaid, and credit report/score through Experian. You can use either or both
  • Dashboard widgets (when enough data is connected): spend by category, spend this month vs trends, upcoming payments, subscriptions, bills, net worth, portfolio distribution, market update for equity holdings, and credit score
  • Credit: Experian credit report and VantageScore 3.0 (300–850). Connecting is a soft inquiry and does not affect your score, per OpenAI. Score/report update monthly after you connect
  • Also read-only for money movement: ChatGPT cannot move money, pay bills, make trades, change retirement contributions, open/close accounts, or file taxes. OpenAI says it is not a fiduciary, RIA, broker-dealer, tax preparer, or law firm
  • Financial memories: goals and context you share (e.g. a planned purchase) can be saved as finance-specific memories and managed from the Finances page

The four buyer decisions that matter

  • 1. Plan gate vs free: Claude Personal Finance requires a paid personal plan in the US. ChatGPT Finances does not. If your only goal is "connect a checking account and ask where the money went," Free ChatGPT is enough on paper. If you already live in Claude Pro for writing or coding, Personal Finance is an included beta feature — not a second bill
  • 2. Privacy defaults: Anthropic hard-codes hub exclusion from training. OpenAI leaves Finances under your global training toggle. Neither product asks for your bank password (both use Plaid). Unlink anytime in-product or at my.plaid.com
  • 3. Credit and investments coverage: need a bureau score and factors → ChatGPT + Experian. need brokerage allocation and daily equity moves → ChatGPT when the brokerage shares it. Claude is stronger as a spending / cash-flow / subscription reader with a clear 24-month transaction window and an explicit no-ads stance
  • 4. Trust and mistakes: both vendors say the AI can be wrong, balances can lag, and you should verify with the bank (and for credit, with Experian). Neither replaces a fiduciary or tax pro. Shared chat links and artifacts on Claude can expose figures from linked accounts — treat share links as sensitive

Which to pick

  • US Claude Pro / Max / Ultra subscriber who wants money questions without training on hub chats: open Personal Finance when it shows up, link one account, ask one concrete question ("What bills hit before payday?")
  • US user who won't pay $20 just for AI budgeting: start on ChatGPT Free Finances; add Experian only if you want the score widget
  • Investor who wants holdings and allocation in the same chat: ChatGPT Finances today; Claude Help is explicit that holdings/trades aren't in scope yet
  • Already on both Pro seats: use Claude for spending narratives and privacy-default hub chats; use ChatGPT when you need credit or portfolio widgets
  • Outside the US, or on Claude Team/Enterprise: Personal Finance isn't available. ChatGPT Finances help is written for the US as well — don't assume either works on a VPN
