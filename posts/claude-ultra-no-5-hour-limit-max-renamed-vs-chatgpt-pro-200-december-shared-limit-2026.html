Claude Max 20x is now Ultra: no 5-hour limit, but the week is 10x Pro. Ultra vs Max vs ChatGPT Pro 200, before OpenAI's December shared limit (2026)

On Friday, Oct 30 (1:29 PM ET), Anthropic posted: "We're renaming our Max plans: Max 5x → Max, Max 20x → Ultra." Prices stay at $100 and $200 a month, and subscribers don't need to do anything. The bigger change is on the $200 plan: Anthropic is "removing the 5-hour limit for verified Ultra accounts, starting today with most existing subscribers," and "weekly limits remain unchanged." The post has passed 6.8 million views. Two minutes later, Mike Overell, who leads product for Claude subscriptions, explained the old name: the top plan launched with a single 5-hour limit at 20x Pro, and "when we added weekly limits to Max 20x, we set them to 10x Pro's." Claude's pricing page now prints that: Ultra = 10x Pro usage every week. The same weekend, OpenAI's Help Center said that starting in December, ChatGPT and Codex will share one usage allowance on Plus, Pro, and Business. We read Anthropic's and OpenAI's pages early on Nov 2.

What Anthropic changed on Oct 30

  • Names: Max 5x is now Max ($100/month). Max 20x is now Ultra ($200/month, web price; Anthropic says mobile pricing may vary). The Next Web reports €90 and €180 in Europe. Both are monthly only
  • Ultra's 5-hour limit is gone for verified accounts. Anthropic verifies accounts automatically "using factors like your account and payment history." Until then, a temporary limit applies, and you'll get an email when it's lifted. Verifying your phone number at checkout "is a separate step and doesn't remove this limit"
  • How to check: go to Settings > Usage. "If you don't see a Current session bar, your account is verified"
  • Weekly limits: "remain unchanged." The pricing page now lists Max at 5x Pro every 5 hours and every week and Ultra at 10x Pro every week. Ultra also keeps a separate weekly limit for Fable
  • Fable: included on Max and Ultra "up to half your weekly limit." On Pro, Fable is paid with usage credits
  • API credits: $100 a month on Max and $200 on Ultra. That's the same amount Max 5x and Max 20x already got, not a new increase
  • Fine print: Anthropic says it "may limit your usage in other ways, such as weekly and monthly caps or model and feature usage," and it doesn't guarantee uninterrupted service. If you have a limit reset, you can use it to refill your weekly limit

Was Max 20x ever 20x a week?

Not by Anthropic's account. Overell wrote that the plan launched in April 2025 with one 5-hour limit set at 20x Pro, and that the weekly limits added later were set at 10x Pro, while "the 5-hour limit stayed at 20x." He added: "I get that some new users assumed 20x applied to both limits. We could have done better here, and I'm sorry." Anthropic's old pricing FAQ tied the multiple to the session ("20x the usage of Pro per 5-hour session") and gave no weekly number. That the week was always 10x rests on Anthropic's word, because no meter shows the limit's actual size. So nobody's week shrank on Oct 30. The name now matches the limit most heavy users actually hit.

Context: Sovereign Magazine reports a proposed class action in the Northern District of California claiming the Max plans delivered less usage than their names implied, and says Anthropic has moved to dismiss it. Overell's post doesn't mention the lawsuit. Separately, users' reports of weekly limits draining faster since mid-October aren't settled by "weekly limits remain unchanged." Meanwhile at OpenAI: one shared pool from December

  • What it says: "Starting in December, Plus, Pro, and Business plans' usage allowance will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex as we bring the agentic capabilities of Work into the main ChatGPT experience." Usage will be measured in tokens, and "more advanced models and complex tasks generally use more of your allowance"
  • What's excluded: "Instant chat will not draw from your usage"
  • OpenAI's reassurance: "We expect most users won't need to wait for their usage to reset more often than they do now." When you hit the limit, you can wait, upgrade, or buy credits
  • What's missing: no exact date and no new allowance sizes. The notice first showed up in ChatGPT's checkout around Oct 30, before the Help Center article; X users have been pushing back all weekend, and some are telling people to cancel before Dec 1. Treat that as opinion. OpenAI hasn't said the pool will be smaller
  • Also this weekend: account switching on mobile (Codex lead Tibo Sottiaux, Oct 31), two "banked" Codex resets on Oct 31 and Nov 1 (check Settings > Usage for one and its expiry), and a Help Center page on card and device limits: ChatGPT may block a new paid subscription if too many are tied to one card or device, and limits how many paid Go, Plus, or Pro accounts you can use on one device

Why it matters for the comparison: Claude already runs chat, desktop, and Claude Code from one pool ("all draws from the same pool," per its pricing FAQ). From December, ChatGPT works the same way, minus Instant chat. If you chat a lot with Thinking models and also run Codex, your Pro week will have to cover both.

Which plan now

  • You hit Claude's 5-hour wall but rarely the weekly one: Ultra is now the fix, as long as your account is verified. Check for the Current session bar before you upgrade; new accounts start with a temporary limit
  • You run out of the week on Max: Ultra doubles the week for double the price. If you only run out sometimes, turning on usage credits (standard API rates) may cost less than another $100
  • You're on Ultra and run out of the week: you were already at the top of Claude's lineup, and that hasn't changed. Your options are usage credits, a limit reset if you have one, or a second tool for overflow. Kimi's plans have no weekly limit: see Kimi K3.1 Pro plans vs Claude Max vs ChatGPT Pro
  • You're picking one $200 plan: Ultra if you live in Claude Code and want Opus 5.5 and Fable (Fable is capped at half the week). Pro 200 if you prefer Codex, want GPT-6 Pro in chat, or want Pro 500 above it
  • You're on ChatGPT Plus and use both chat and Codex: watch December most closely. Plus has the smallest pool to share. Pro 100 is about 10x Plus for 5x the price
