GPT-6 and Intelligent UI are in ChatGPT for everyone: what Free, Go, Plus, and Pro actually get, and when Claude or Gemini is the better $20 (2026)

On Oct 7, 2026, OpenAI started rolling out GPT-6 with Intelligent UI in ChatGPT. Answers can now mix text with charts, diagrams, tappable buttons, forms, and small tools (calculators, bill splitters, even games) built right inside the chat, and GPT-6 can start answering while it’s still thinking. Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise got it first; Free and Go followed starting Oct 8. OpenAI’s launch post on X passed 5.5 million views within a day. The catch the hype skips: “GPT-6 for everyone” is not the same model for everyone. Paid plans get GPT-6 Sol; Free and Go get the lighter, cheaper Luna. And Plus doesn’t get the top thinking levels. We read OpenAI’s release notes and Help Center on the morning of Oct 9.

What OpenAI actually shipped (Oct 7–8)

  • Intelligent UI: GPT-6 composes answers from text, visuals, and interactive elements, and picks the format itself. You don’t need a special prompt, and it still answers in plain text when that fits best
  • Answers start sooner: GPT-6 can begin responding while it keeps thinking or using tools, then add findings without another prompt. OpenAI says GPT-6 Instant starts answering web-search questions 44% sooner on average than GPT-5.6 Instant (time to first answer, not total time)
  • Rollout: Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise from Oct 7 in the Chat tab; Free and Go from Oct 8. Enterprise depends on admin settings
  • Which model you get: Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise use GPT-6 Sol. Free and Go use Luna. (OpenAI’s release notes say “GPT-6 Luna”; its models Help Center article still describes Free and Go as getting GPT-5.6 Luna. Either way, it’s the lighter model, not Sol)
  • Where it works: on the web and updated mobile apps, at Instant through Extra High. Not with Pro effort, which stays on GPT-6 Pro (Astra). The older ChatGPT desktop apps for macOS and Windows don’t support it, so use the web
  • Limits: Intelligent UI has no separate quota; your plan’s normal model and tool limits apply. To tone it down, open Settings on the web and turn off “Layout and visuals” (some visuals may still appear)
  • Not changing: the models behind ChatGPT Work and Codex. Older selectable models aren’t retired, and an explicit GPT-5.6 Sol selection stays on GPT-5.6
  • Same week: OpenAI added a $500/month Pro 500 plan with Astra Ultrafast in Work and Codex, audio uploads for paid plans, and Finances for Free and Go users in the U.S.

Checklist before you upgrade

  1. Use it free first. Ask ChatGPT for a comparison, a calculator, or a diagram on the web. If the visual answers are what you wanted, Free or Go may be all you need.
  2. Check the model menu. On paid plans, look for “GPT-6” (it isn’t labeled Sol). If it’s missing, the rollout hasn’t reached your account yet.
  3. Plus vs Pro is about thinking, not visuals. Intelligent UI works the same from Instant through Extra High. Pay for Pro only if you need Extra High, GPT-6 Pro, or more Codex and Work headroom.
  4. Desktop app users: the older macOS and Windows apps don’t show Intelligent UI. Switch to the web or update.
  5. Find it distracting? Turn off “Layout and visuals” in Settings on the web before you cancel anything.
  6. Compare on your real work. Run one writing task and one coding task in ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro side by side before committing to either $20.
