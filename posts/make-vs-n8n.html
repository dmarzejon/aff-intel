Make.com vs n8n Cloud

Automation platforms turn SaaS glue work into repeatable scenarios. Make and n8n are the two most common picks after Zapier.

Quick verdict

Make.com wins for visual speed and polished SaaS connectors. n8n Cloud wins for self-host option, code nodes, and cost control at volume.

When to choose Make

Pick Make if non-engineers will own the workflows, you need many SaaS templates, and you prefer a managed cloud experience.

When to choose n8n

Pick n8n if you want JavaScript/Python in the middle of a flow, complex branching, or the option to self-host for data residency and unit cost.
